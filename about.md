I'm DP — a builder, thinker, and occasional writer based on the internet.

This is my corner of the web where I write about technology, design, and the ideas that make modern life interesting. I believe good writing is a form of clear thinking, and I'm trying to get better at both.

## What you'll find here

Most of my writing falls somewhere in the overlap between:

- **Technology** — how software shapes our world, and how we shape it back
- **Design** — the craft of making things that are both useful and beautiful
- **Ideas** — the kind of half-formed thoughts that become useful once written down

## A bit more

I've spent time building products, thinking about systems, and reading more books than I have time for. I'm drawn to questions that don't have clean answers.

If something I've written resonated with you, I'd love to hear about it.
